Virtual Balloon

Virtual Balloon is a game of nerve: hold a key to blow up a balloon, and do not let go.
It is a remake of Virtual Balloon, which On Beat Limited made for the iPhone in 2009, and it plays the original's own sounds, with the same timings.
Everything the game says goes through your screen reader, or through a Windows voice when no screen reader is running.

Starting the game

Run VirtualBalloon.exe in the VirtualBalloon-Windows folder.
The game plays On Beat's logo and its jingle, then the title, with its music.
The title tells you the keys.

How to play

Hold Space, and the balloon starts to fill with air, while the music slowly fades away.
The game says "Inflating...".
At 16, 26 and 32 seconds the balloon squeaks, from the center, the left and the right, as if it were about to burst.
It is only a bluff, so keep holding.
If you let go too soon, the air goes out of the balloon for as long as you held on, and the music comes back.
The game tells you how many seconds you held on, and you can hold Space again to try again.
If you hold on to the end, at about 39 seconds, you win: the balloon pops, and a second later the crowd cheers.
Press Space as soon as the cheer starts to play again, and the game says "Ready".
Then hold Space to start a new round.
The balloon always inflates for at least a fifth of a second, however quickly you tap, and rests a fifth of a second after you let go, so tapping Space very fast does not do anything.

Keys

Space: hold to blow up the balloon; press to try again, or to play again after a win.
H: say a quick help.
A: turn the game's announcements off or on. The game always says which, and it remembers your choice.
Escape: quit.
H and A do nothing while the logo plays.

What is in the folder

VirtualBalloon.exe is the game.
The game folder holds the original game's files: its scenes, its actors, its pictures and its sounds.
The game only plays the sounds, but the rest is there for you to look at.
The docks folder holds this readme and the changelog, which lists what each version changed.
VERSION says which version this is, and license.txt is the game's license.

Settings

The game keeps its one setting, whether the announcements are on, in the Windows registry, under HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\tsatria03\VirtualBalloon.
It never writes anywhere else, so a new version can go in a fresh folder.
