The original (2009, made with GameSalad, an iPhone-only game) keeps its rules in XML files. Timings, in seconds after the key goes down:
- The inflating sound (`balloon_inflate.ogg`, 39.8 s) starts on the press; it was played as GameSalad "music", so it keeps playing across scene changes.
- Squeaks at 16 (center), 26 (left) and 32 (right) seconds - a bluff, nothing happens.
- Let go early: the inflating sound stops, the deflating sound plays, and the seconds held are shown (VirtualBalloon.py speaks them).
- Hold to 39 s: the win screen appears; nothing stops the inflating sound, so **the balloon pops at about 39.1-39.5 s** (the pop is inside the inflating sound's last moments, not a separate file or rule). The crowd cheers 1 s into the win screen (40 s); a new round is accepted only 4 s after the win (43 s).
- The user confirmed the pop by ear at about 38-39 s (2026-10-16). Earlier I wrongly said the balloon never pops: I had read only the rules, not listened to the sound - the audio files are part of the behaviour.
- `music.ogg` is the original's unused `piano.ogg`; in the remake it's the title music.
- **The remake's win is at 38.75 s, not 39** (2026-10-16): the user trimmed 0.25 s of silence from the start of `balloon_inflate.ogg` (39.82 -> 39.57 s), which moved the pop to about 38.8-39.3 s, so `WIN_AT` moved 0.25 s earlier to keep the pop just after the win. Cheer at 39.75 s, and Space can play again from then on (see "Skipping the cheer"). If the sound's start is edited again, move `WIN_AT` by the same amount (or tie the win to the sound's end - option 2, not chosen). Pop found by bytes per tenth of a second (scratchpad `ogg-burst.py`), confirmed earlier by the user's ear.

How VirtualBalloon.py copies it (pygame + accessible_output2):
- `Round` holds the rules and takes the time as a number (testable); `Game` plays sounds and speaks.
- Two reserved channels: one for the music, one for the balloon. `Channel.play` replaces what the channel was playing, so inflating and deflating never overlap.
- Music: loops from the title on, at **-10 dB** (`MUSIC_DB`; on 2026-10-16 the user tried full volume, 0 dB, then went back to -10: "I liked it better when it was quieter"), and keeps playing between tries. It stops at the win and comes back at the next round.
- While you hold Space, the music fades out steadily (in dB) from where it was to -100 dB over the inflating sound's length (pygame reads it as 39.55 s), so it's gone by the pop (about -98 dB at the 39 s win). When you let go, the deflating sound plays only as long as you held Space (at most its full 6.29 s), then fades out in 30 ms (`DEFLATE_CUT_FADE_MS`, `Game.cut_deflate`; user: "if you let it go after 1 second, it should only play for that amount of time"). The music fades steadily back in from where it was to -10 dB over that same time (`Game.deflate_length`), however deep it was - the user first asked for "the length of the balloon deflate sound", which is now the shortened length. Pressing again mid-way starts the fade-out from the current level, so there's no jump. Functions: `fade_db(start_db, end_db, elapsed, length)` and `Game.fade_music(now, seconds)`. This is the user's "brand new strategy" of 2026-10-16; before that the music ducked to a fixed level (-20, -30, then -25 dB).
- **Mixer quirk (pygame 2.6.1, SDL_mixer 2.6.2):** if a new sound starts on a channel while `Channel.fadeout()` is running, the fade stops but the channel KEEPS the volume the fade had reached, permanently, and it adds up over cycles (measured 0.664, then 0.266 after 5 fast cut/restart cycles). The user heard it as "the balloon sounds get effected by the music volume" (it only happened while pressing/releasing, when the music fades too). Fix: `Game.play_balloon()` always calls `self.balloon.set_volume(1.0)` after `play()`. Any new sound channel that uses fadeout needs the same.
- Volumes are written in decibels, converted with 10^(dB/20) (the same formula the user's usual game engine uses for its `volume` property).
- Keys: hold Space to inflate, H speaks a quick help (`Game.help()`, the text is `game.HELP`; always spoken even with announcements off, ignored during the logo; user 2026-10-16), A turns announcements off/on, Escape quits. The title mentions H and A.
- Announcements (6): "On Beat presents"; the title (mentions Space, A and Escape); "Inflating..."; "You let go after ... Hold Space to try again."; "Well done! ... Press Space to play again."; "Ready. Hold Space to blow up the balloon." All go through `Game.say()`, which checks `self.announcements`. The A key (`toggle_announcements`, user 2026-10-16) always speaks "Announcements off" / "Announcements on" directly, so muting is confirmed. A does nothing while the On Beat logo plays (state "logo"; user request, 2026-10-16). The setting is saved in the registry (user, 2026-10-16: "saved to the windows reg"): `HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\tsatria03\VirtualBalloon`, DWORD value `Announcements` (1 on, 0 off), via `load_setting()`/`save_setting()` with `winreg`; no saved value means on. Tests must point `SETTINGS_KEY` at a separate test key and delete it, never write the real key.
- Skipping the cheer (user, 2026-10-16): after a win, Space works again as soon as the cheer starts (`Round.can_restart` returns `cheered`), 1 s after the win; it used to wait 4 s after the win like the original. The press goes to Ready and stops the cheer.
- 200 ms cooldown (`COOLDOWN = 0.2`; first 500 ms after the user said "you can spam inflate/deflate the balloon", then 100 ms, then 200 ms, all at the user's request on 2026-10-16): the balloon inflates at least 0.2 s before letting go counts (`Round.update`), and after letting go a new try can't start for 0.2 s (`Game.try_again`).
- The inflating sound starts with about 0.2 s of very quiet sound (-41 to -55 dB) before the real inflating (-34 dB and up); the user decided NOT to trim it (2026-10-16). The squeaks are very quiet on purpose or by the original (peaks -34 to -45 dB, vs -6 dB for the inflating sound); the user trimmed the left squeak's quiet start (4.0 -> 3.5 s, audible from 0.41 s instead of 0.89 s). A press during the rest isn't lost: if Space is still held when the rest ends, the new try starts then (checked every frame). Letting go in under 1 s says "You let go after less than a second." (the user asked to change the earlier "0 seconds"); from 1 s on it says whole seconds ("1 second", "23 seconds").
- Playing again (since 2026-10-16, user: pressing Space to play again "shouldn't imideatly make you blow up the balloon"): 4 s after a WIN, a Space press goes to the "ready" state ("Ready. Hold Space to blow up the balloon.", music back on), and only the next press starts a round. After a LOSS there is no Ready step: the next press starts a new try at once (user: Ready "should only occur after winning, not after you release too soon"). Key presses are handled in `Game.press_space(now)`; pygame's key repeat is off, so holding Space never counts as a second press.

**Why:** these numbers came from the original game files; keep them unless the user asks for a change.
**How to apply:** check new changes against this list, update it when the design changes, and add each change to [[changes-file-style]].
