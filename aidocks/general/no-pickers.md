Never use the AskUserQuestion tool (option pickers). Ask questions as plain text at the end of the reply, with any options as a short numbered list (say which one you recommend), and wait for the typed answer. **Only one question per reply.**

**Why:** the user asked for questions without pickers, and one at a time; they use NVDA ([[screen-reader-user]]), and plain-text questions are easier for them.
**How to apply:** when a decision is needed, ask the most important question, stop, and ask the next one after the answer.
